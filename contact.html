Before you call

Questions we get every week

Do you charge brokerage?

Not on new projects — the developer pays us. On resale properties the brokerage is agreed with you in writing before any paperwork starts.

How quickly can I see a property?

Usually within 48 hours, often the same evening. Site visits are free and we do not ask for anything up front.

Can you help with a home loan?

Yes. We prepare the file with you and take it to the lenders we work with. There is no fee for it, and no obligation to use them.

I want to sell. What do you need from me?

A call, then a visit. We photograph and measure the property ourselves and come back to you with a price we can defend.

Do you handle rentals?

For office space and select residential properties, yes. Call us and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right people for it.
