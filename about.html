About Estatly

A small team that still visits every home

We started in 2011 with one office and a simple rule we have never broken: nothing goes on the site until one of us has stood inside it.

Our story

Built by people who got tired of wasted site visits

Estatly began after a weekend spent driving across the city to see four flats that looked nothing like their photos. We thought the listing itself should do the honest work, so we built an agency around that.

Fifteen years later the rule has not changed. Our advisors photograph, measure and document each property themselves, and the price you read is the price the seller quoted us — nothing added on top.

  • Every listing visited in person before it goes live
  • No brokerage on new projects, ever
  • One advisor stays with you from first call to handover

How we work

Four things we will not compromise on

We see it first

Photos, measurements and paperwork are checked on site, not forwarded from the seller.

We quote one number

The price on the card is the price on the agreement. No hidden brokerage, no surprises at signing.

We say no

If a property is wrong for you we will tell you, even when it costs us the sale.

We finish the job

Registration, loan paperwork and handover are part of the service, not an extra.
